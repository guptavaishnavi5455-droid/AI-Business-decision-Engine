\# AI-Powered Business Decision Engine



\## Project Description



The AI-Powered Business Decision Engine is a decision-support application that analyzes a business idea using financial, market, competition and scalability factors.



The system calculates important business metrics such as monthly profit, profit margin and break-even period. It also uses a Random Forest machine-learning model to predict business viability and provides risk factors, personalized recommendations and a final business decision.



\## Problem Statement



Business decisions require analysis of multiple factors such as investment, revenue, expenses, market demand, competition and scalability. For beginners and small business planners, evaluating these factors together can be difficult.



This project provides an interactive AI-assisted system that brings these factors together and presents the analysis in an easy-to-understand dashboard.



\## Objectives



\- Analyze business financial performance.

\- Calculate monthly profit and profit margin.

\- Estimate the break-even period.

\- Evaluate demand, competition and scalability.

\- Predict business viability using machine learning.

\- Identify potential business risks.

\- Generate personalized recommendations.

\- Provide a downloadable business analysis report.

\- Revenue

\- Expenses

\- Market Demand

\- Competition

\- Scalability



The model predicts business viability based on the provided business information.
